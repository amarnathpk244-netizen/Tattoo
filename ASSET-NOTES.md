# Asset provenance

Images were created with the built-in image generation tool for this website concept. They are illustrative demo imagery, not real studio/client photographs. They are copied into `assets/` and the deployable `dist/assets/`; the portable HTML embeds copies.

## assets/studio-session.png

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website hero photograph for a premium tattoo studio concept. Create a vertical 4:5 editorial black-and-white photograph, close-up at an oblique angle of a tattoo artist's black nitrile gloved hands working with a tattoo pen on an adult client's forearm, an intricate botanical blackwork tattoo visible on the forearm. No blood, no wound, no text, no watermark, no logos. Quiet dark studio backdrop, strong soft window light catching real skin texture, dark clothing, rich charcoal shadows, fine analog grain, sophisticated art magazine photography, shallow depth of field. Frame the hands and forearm in the middle and lower center with generous dark space around them. Do not show a face. Anatomically natural hands, realistic clean professional equipment. This is clearly illustrative concept imagery for a fictional studio.

## assets/portfolio-studies.png

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: demo tattoo portfolio contact sheet for a fictional tattoo studio website. A perfectly regular 3-column by 2-row edge-to-edge grid of six separate equal rectangular black-and-white editorial tattoo photographs with no gutters, no borders, no labels, no text, no watermarks. Each cell contains a DIFFERENT adult's arm/shoulder with a beautifully detailed finished black ink tattoo on clean skin against dark neutral gray background. Top left: fine-line botanical fern branch on inner forearm. Top middle: bold blackwork moth with symmetrical wings on upper arm. Top right: delicate ornamental celestial compass on forearm. Bottom left: intricate realistic rose flower on upper arm. Bottom middle: abstract flowing black ink curves on shoulder, fully clothed elsewhere. Bottom right: fine-line snake winding around an olive branch on forearm. Restrained contrast, real skin textures, editorial close crops, no faces, no blood, no wound, no extraneous body parts. Keep each tattoo subject centered within its own grid cell; consistent tonal treatment. These are illustrative concept samples, not real client work.

The portfolio renderer uses CSS background positions to show the six contact-sheet cells. The raster images are unaltered from generation. Typography is a locally bundled subset of DejaVu Sans Bold, with its original font notice included. The favicon is a simple code-created star mark in SVG.
